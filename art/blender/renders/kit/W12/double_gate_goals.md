# W12 DoubleGate goals

## ASSEMBLED / HERO
1. [must] Silhouette: closed double gate ~16 wide x 12 tall (width:height ~1.3), two chunky square posts at the ends, a heavy lintel across the top. — MET - hero/front: two square posts + heavy lintel, 17.7x12 (incl. rear knee braces), reads ~1.4:1 like the reference
2. [must] Lintel overhangs past both posts with stepped/offset end blocks; posts have a smaller block cap poking above the lintel line. — MET - lintel end blocks step out past both posts and a two-tier block cap pokes above the lintel on each post
3. [must] Two closed leaves fill the 10 x 8 doorway, each of 4-5 vertical planks with a visible central seam. — MET - front cell: two leaves of 4 vertical planks with uneven tops and a clear centre seam
4. [must] Each leaf has 2 dark iron horizontal straps and a black ring handle near the centre seam at ~z4. — MET - two dark straps with rivets per leaf, black rings either side of the seam at mid height
5. [must] One red swallowtail banner (1.3 x 4) with a cream diamond on the front of each post. — MET - red swallowtail banner with enlarged cream diamond on the front of each post
6. [must] A lantern with glowing amber glass hangs beside each banner on the inner side of each post at z~6-7.5; glass visibly emissive. — MET - yellow-amber glowing lanterns on the inner side of each banner, glass clearly emissive in hero/front
7. [must] Grey boulders (1.5-3 studs) at both post feet with green clover tufts around them. — MET - grey boulders up to 2.6 studs at both post feet with green clover tufts (tufts a little smaller than reference)
8. [should] Tan rope wraps near the top of each post / at lintel junctions. — MET - tan double rope band under the lintel on each post plus lashings on the lintel
9. [should] Iron hinge blocks visible on the outer edges of the leaves. — MET - iron hinge straps/pins visible on the outer leaf edges against the posts

## FRONT
10. [must] Front view reads symmetric: post-banner-lantern | two leaves | lantern-banner-post, lintel ends overhanging. — MET - front cell symmetric: banner+lantern|leaves|lantern+banner with overhanging lintel ends
11. [should] Front view shows ~4 planks per leaf with straps spanning the full leaf width. — MET - 4 planks per leaf, straps span the leaf width

## SIDE / REAR
12. [must] Rear view shows diagonal wooden braces from the posts to the ground and the latch bar across the leaf rears. — MET - rear cell shows iron straps on the leaf backs, latch bar across the seam and outward diagonal knee braces (braces read smaller than in reference)

## TOP
13. [should] Top view: long lintel bar with the square post caps and end blocks at both ends, boulders peeking out at the feet. — MET - top cell: long lintel with end blocks and post caps, stones/clover peeking at the feet

## PARTS
14. [must] Nine parts present and recognisable: upright post (rope wrap, chamfered top), top beam with stepped ends, hinge block+pin, gate leaf with straps+ring, latch bar, latch peg, rope wrap coil, ring handle with plate, red banner on crossbar. — MET - parts cell shows post with rope + cap, stepped beam, hinge block+pin, leaf with straps+ring, latch bar, peg, rope coil, ring+plate, banner on crossbar

## VARIATIONS
15. [must] A. Closed: same gate, only one banner+lantern pair, no boulders on the left foot. — MET - Closed: single banner+lantern on left post, boulder/clover only on the right foot
16. [must] B. Open: both leaves swung ~100 deg, standing perpendicular beside the posts, doorway clear 10 x 8. — MET - Open: leaves swung 95 deg inward standing behind the posts, doorway fully clear
17. [must] C. Ruined: right post intact with banner+lantern, left post broken at half height and leaning, lintel snapped with half on the ground, one leaf crooked, broken planks on the ground. — MET - Ruined: right post with banner+lantern, left stump snapped at ~5, right lintel half sagging, half lying on ground, crooked leaf, planks/brace debris

## PALETTE
18. [must] Timber is one warm orange-brown (w12_wood #8F5A2C) on posts, lintel, planks, braces; render matches reference's saturated mid-brown. — MET - all timber one w12_wood; render orange-brown matches the reference timber
19. [must] Iron (w12_iron #3B404C) dark blue-grey only on straps, hinges, ring handles and lantern frames; banner red #C8202B with cream #F2E6D2 diamond; rope tan #C9985A; stone #7E7F8C; leaf #3F9A2E. — MET - iron only on straps/hinges/rings/lantern frames; banner red + cream diamond, tan rope, grey stone, green clover

## VFX
20. [must] heat_glow sockets at each lantern on DoubleGate, GateClosed (1), GateOpen (2), GateRuined (1). — MET - stats: heat_glow sockets 2/1/2/1 on DoubleGate/GateClosed/GateOpen/GateRuined at lantern glass

## Iteration log
- Build 1: full layout; rear braces in YZ were invisible from the rear, dressing pushed width to 18.2, rocks small.
- Build 2: braces moved to outward knee braces on the rear (visible in rear cell), bigger boulders + 5 clover per foot, two-tier post caps.
- Build 3: iron straps added on leaf backs (rear cell now matches), banner diamond enlarged, ruined variant got leaning snapped plank + extra debris. All musts met; stopped.

# V01 DandelionRosette goals

Reference: art/reference/kit/rows/V01_row4.png. Status after each build is in [brackets].

## ASSEMBLED / HERO
1. (must) Rosette of 9-11 broad lance leaves radiating from the centre; footprint ~3.8 x 3.8 studs. [MET - 11 leaves radiate, star footprint 4.0x3.8]
2. (must) Leaves have jagged, toothed (pinnate, backward-pointing lobe) edges visible in silhouette, 4-6 teeth per side on large leaves. [MET - sawtooth backward lobes read in front/side silhouettes]
3. (must) Each leaf shows a central midrib ridge (folded V cross-section). [UNMET - crease fold exists but is not readable at render scale]
4. (must) Lower leaves lie nearly flat and curl down at the tips; upper/inner leaves rise at ~40-60 deg. [MET - low leaves lie near flat, alternate leaves rise ~55 deg]
5. (must) One chunky yellow flower head ~1 stud wide, flat-topped, two layers of faceted petal wedges, on top of a straight thick stalk; total height ~3.2. [MET - chunky 3-tier flat-topped yellow head on straight stalk, 3.3 tall]
6. (must) Small closed green bud with a yellow tip on a short side stalk (~1.3 tall) low on one side of the main stalk. [UNMET - bud with yellow tip present right of stalk but reads too small, half hidden in leaves]
7. (should) Small brown root knot visible at the hub with short splayed roots at ground level. [UNMET - root knot mostly hidden under leaves]
8. (should) Hero overall about as wide as tall (width/height ~1.1-1.2). [MET - 4.0 wide x 3.3 tall, wide leafy base like reference]

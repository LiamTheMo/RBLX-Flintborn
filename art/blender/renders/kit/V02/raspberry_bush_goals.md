4. Build 4: less droop, lowest elevation raised to -30 deg, longer leaves, narrower shell, berries raised and pushed further out. Hero shows 4 clumps. Still about 7.4 wide. This was the build limit.
Open: hero width is about 1.2x the target; the top view shows only 2 berry clumps; leaf green is darker than the reference because the palette is fixed.

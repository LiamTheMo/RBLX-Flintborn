# R08 Mooncap goals

## ASSEMBLED / HERO
1. [must] Two mushrooms: big one left/centre, smaller one to the right and partly behind it; small cap ~0.6x the big cap width.
2. [must] Big cap is a flattened faceted dome ~7.5 w x 6 d x 3.5 h that overhangs the stem on all sides like an umbrella (cap width ~2.7x stem top width).
3. [must] Big cap tilts slightly toward the viewer's right (left rim visibly higher than right rim).
4. [must] Caps read as one mid/dark violet purple (r08_cap_purple #6248B2), angular large facets (roughly 10-20 visible), no smooth look.
5. [must] Each cap carries 4-6 bright glowing cyan spot discs (r08_spot_cyan), irregular placement and sizes (0.8-1.8), biggest on the top, a couple on the side, sitting slightly proud.
6. [must] Stems are fat, tapered (wider at base), faceted 7-8 sides and cream (r08_stem_cream), the lightest material in the render.
7. [must] Base: ring of 8-10 chunky grey-brown r08_rock boulders (~1.2-2.2) around the stems, visible in front.
8. [should] 2 small r08_leaf_green moss tufts at the left of the big stem, low between rocks.
9. [should] Hero overall ~10 w x 8 d x 8 h studs (width ~1.25x height).

## FRONT
10. [must] Front view: big cap dome silhouette wide and low, stem visible below it, small mushroom peeking out at right behind the big stem.
11. [should] Front view shows 2-3 cyan spots on the big cap face, one big one near the top.

## SIDE
12. [should] Side view: cap overhang still clear; small mushroom visible behind/right with its cap lower than big cap.

## TOP
13. [must] Top view: two overlapping roughly round purple caps (big + small) covering most of the rock ring, rocks peeking out at the rim; 4-6 cyan spots on each.

## PARTS
14. [must] MooncapCapShell: lone purple faceted dome ~7.5x6x3.5, no spots.
15. [must] MooncapSpotInsert: lone faceted cyan gem/disc ~1.6x1.6x0.8, glowing.
16. [must] MooncapStem: lone cream tapered stem ~2.8x2.8x3.5.
17. [should] MooncapBase: rock pile with dirt, ~9x7x2.2, grey-brown.

## DROPS / DEPLETED
18. [must] MooncapDrop: small mushroom ~2.2 w x 2.2 h, purple cap with two cyan spots on a cream stub, <=300 tris.
19. [must] SporePod: mint-green (r08_spore) faceted teardrop ~1.4x1.4x2.2, <=300 tris.
20. [must] MooncapDepleted: rock ring with dirt centre, two short cream stem stumps (~1.6 and ~1.1 tall), no caps.

## Palette
21. [must] Only sheet keys used: r08_cap_purple, r08_spot_cyan, r08_stem_cream, r08_rock, r08_leaf_green, r08_spore; one colour per material.

## VFX
22. [should] glyph_glow + cyan light on each cap, faint spore motes at (0,0,6.5), harvest_hit (0,-3,5), harvest_deplete on depleted; spot insert glyph_glow.

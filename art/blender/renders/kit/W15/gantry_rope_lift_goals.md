# W15 row 4 GantryRopeLift goals

## ASSEMBLED / HERO
1. [must] Two tall square timber uprights (~1.6 x 1.6, ~15 tall) on grey rock footings, joined by a long cross beam across the top that overhangs both posts; hero ~14 wide x ~18 tall (taller than wide, ~1.2-1.3x).
2. [must] Diagonal braces at both top inner corners (post to cross beam), reading as a timber A-frame/gantry.
3. [must] Iron pulley wheel (dark blue-grey, ~2 dia) hung under the cross beam on an iron bracket, near the centre.
4. [must] Thick rope-tan rope runs over the pulley in two strands: one down to a ring with 4 lines to an open plank crate, the other to an iron counterweight block; crate hangs clear of the deck (gap >= 2 studs).
5. [must] Timber deck with post-and-rail fence (2 rails, ~1.3 tall) at the base on the left/front.
6. [must] Red banner with a pale diamond hangs on the right upright below the beam.
7. [must] 3-5 big grey faceted rocks (~6-8 tall) behind/beside the frame, plus rock footings.
8. [must] 8+ bright green leafy tufts at rock bases and deck sides; a sandy patch under the asset.
9. [should] Iron plates with bolt heads on the cross-beam ends and post joints.

## FRONT
10. [must] Front view: symmetric frame, beam overhanging posts by ~2 on each side, pulley at centre, crate centred below, counterweight to the right of the crate.
## SIDE
11. [should] Side view shows the post tall and slim with the crate/rope hanging beside it and a raking strut/brace.
## TOP
12. [should] Top view: long beam across the width, two post squares, crate square visible below the beam centre.

## PARTS
13. [must] Six separate parts: CrossBeam (~12 long, iron end plates + bolts), UprightPost (~14 on rock footing), DiagonalBrace (~5 angled, notched ends), PulleyWheel (octagonal ~2 dia, hub, bracket stub), ThickRope (rope-tan coil ~1.5 dia), CargoPlatform (open crate ~4x4x2.5 with iron corner plates and 4 ropes to a ring).

## VARIATIONS
14. [must] ShortCrane: one post on rocks, outstretched beam with brace, rope with small crate frame above a fenced deck, ~10 tall.
15. [must] HangingBasket: single upright + beam, iron pulley block, rope-woven cage basket with timber rims hanging clear, rock base with tufts.
16. [must] TallHoist: ~24 tall, two posts, top cross beam, fenced middle platform deck, rope + pulley, red banner, hanging cargo, rock base.

## PALETTE
17. [must] Timber is ONE warm orange-brown (#A9662B) on all wood; rope is visibly lighter golden tan (#C0903A), not timber orange.
18. [must] Iron is one dark blue-grey (#454B66) on pulley, plates, bolts, counterweight, crate corners; rock one neutral grey (#8E8E96).
19. [must] Foliage one vivid green (#3E9A22), banner one red (#C8161F), emblem/ground sand (#D4B98E).

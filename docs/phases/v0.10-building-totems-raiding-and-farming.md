# v0.10 — Building, totems, raiding and farming

> **Depends on:** v0.07 (tribes), v0.08 (portal zones) · **GDD sources:** §6 (all), §22.4, §32.2–32.3, §35.2 Q34–41, §38.3 Q113–114, §39.4 Q144–146, Q159a–b

## Objective

Add Booga Booga-style base building that lives only as long as its world. Tribe totems stop natural decay, never raids. Bases stay after their owners leave and can be raided by anyone at any time with ordinary tools. Farms keep growing while the world is populated.

## Player-facing result

- A build menu with **placement preview, quick rotate and snap** for walls, floors/foundations, doors, roofs, storage chests, beds, workbenches, furnaces/smelters, traps and planters, in **wood → stone → iron** tiers.
- **Tribe totems**, starting with a hut-sized claim. **Upgrading** the totem grows its radius and raises its HP through **500 → 1,000 → 2,500**.
- **Unclaimed structures disappear completely 4–6 minutes** after losing totem cover.
- **Storage chests open for anyone** who can physically reach them.
- **Raid any time** by hitting structures with normal weapons and tools.
- **Farms:** plant crops and trees that keep growing while the owner is away, as long as the world has players.
- **Takeover:** structures under a standing totem cannot be taken over. Destroy the totem, and another tribe can claim the buildings by placing its own.

## Confirmed rules

- **Accessible building** (Q34), with the object list above (Q35), in **wood → stone → iron** tiers (Q36).
- Bases **stay after owners leave** (Q37) and **vanish when the world empties** (Q2). There are **no cross-server bases**.
- **Totems:**
  - start at about **hut size**, and **material upgrades grow the radius** (Q144, Q159a);
  - **HP 500 / 1,000 / 2,500** by level (Q159a);
  - prevent **decay only, never raids** (§6.2);
  - claim only **player-built** structures, **never server-generated** ones (Q39).
- **Overlap:** totem areas may overlap, including rival tribes; the **original owner keeps ownership** until a valid takeover (Q145).
- **Takeover:** **impossible while a covering totem stands** (Q159b). After it falls, the structures are unclaimed (decaying) and **another tribe claims them by placing its own totem** (§42.2 #1 Confirmed).
- **Unclaimed decay:** each structure **fully disappears 4–6 minutes** after losing cover, using a random lifetime of 240–360 s per structure. It is removed outright, not damaged in ticks (Q146). Reclaiming stops it without timer flicker.
- **Raiding:** **any time**, including with owners offline; **no special raid tools required** (Q38). Optional future siege tools may speed raids but never become mandatory (§6.3).
- **Storage is public** to anyone who can reach it. There is no owner-only chest on the island; only the vault is safe (Q39).
- **Beds are furniture, not respawn points** (§6.1).
- **Farming:** plant and grow crops and trees that grow **while the server stays populated**, using server-side growth timestamps (Q41).
- **No building inside the 50 m aura** around extraction portals and runes. Checks use the whole piece's geometry, not just its centre (§3.1, §22.4).

## Data and persistence

None persistent: all structures, chests and farms are world state and are discarded when the world empties. In-server structure records hold `structureId`, `ownerTribeId`, `tier`, `hp`, `coveredByTotemId`, `decayDeadline`.

## Security and anti-exploit

From GDD §6.2 anti-exploit and §24:

- cheap totems near boss or portal no-build zones are rejected;
- negative-price or free radius upgrades are rejected;
- claim spoofing and repeated decay-timer resets are blocked;
- placing pieces that overlap a portal aura, even partially, is rejected;
- structure damage comes only from CombatService hits, with per-weapon structure damage;
- storage access requires the player to physically reach the chest; walls block it until breached.

## UX and accessibility

- The preview shows valid and invalid placement with colour **and** an icon.
- The 50 m no-build aura is visible while the build tool is equipped (§3.2).
- Totem radius and level, structure HP and decay countdowns are shown on hover/focus.
- Building works on touch with tap-to-place and rotate buttons, without precise dragging (§25.4).

## Validation

**Automated specs (`BuildRules`):**
- the aura rejects partial overlaps;
- server-generated structures are never claimable;
- coverage stops decay and loss of cover assigns a 240–360 s deadline;
- totem HP by level is 500/1,000/2,500;
- overlapping claims keep the original owner;
- takeover is impossible while a covering totem stands.

**Studio checks (GDD §32.3, §35.4 #11–14, §39.6 #12–13):**
- Build wood → stone → iron.
- The owner leaves and the base remains.
- A raider breaks walls and loots a chest.
- Remove the totem: the structures vanish within 4–6 minutes.
- Destroy a rival totem, place your own and claim the base.
- Crops grow while the farmer is away.
- The world empties and everything resets.

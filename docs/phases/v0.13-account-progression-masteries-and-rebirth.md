# v0.13 — Account progression, masteries and rebirth

> **Depends on:** v0.08 · **GDD sources:** §9.3, §33 (all), §36.3–36.4, §37 (all), §39.5, §40.1–40.2 Q153, Q52–54, Q69–89, Q139, Q150

## Objective

Give every session permanent progress:
- general XP and levels that save the moment they are earned;
- five capped mastery tracks with a buff at every level;
- a once-only Center Crater XP doubling;
- a rebirth system that resets level for permanent perk tokens;
- deterministic stacking of every XP and yield modifier.

## Player-facing result

- XP pop-ups from harvesting, crafting, events, PvP and quests. A level bar in the HUD and lobby.
- Five **mastery tracks**: **Mining, Woodcutting, Farming, Crafting, PvP**. Each goes to **level 10** with a buff at every level, plus cosmetic and title milestones visible in the lobby.
- A Center Crater extraction receipt that shows **run XP and an equal bonus**.
- A **rebirth** screen: requirements (level and an endgame challenge), a preview of exactly what resets (general level only) and what is kept, the token award, and a **perk shop** of permanent, always-active perks.

## Confirmed rules

**XP and levels**
- **XP, levels and crafting mastery; no skill trees** (Q52).
- **XP and mastery progress save immediately when earned**, even if the player later dies or fails to extract (Q70).
- **Center Crater extraction** adds a bonus **equal to all valid XP earned in that run**, general and mastery, **once** per receipt. It never doubles prior runs and never adds mastery action counts (Q69, §9.3, §33.2).
- **Portable runes never grant the bonus** (Q158).

**Masteries**
- **Exactly five tracks** (Q71); **cap 10** (Q73); **a buff every level** (Q74).
- Progress is **milestone and action based**, not time played (Q72).
- **Mining, Woodcutting and Farming** give **+1% yield per level**, so +10% at level 10 (Q75).
- **Crafting** gives all four benefit types: ingredient savings, larger outputs, faster crafting and recipe unlocks (Q77). It also raises the best-roll chance by 5% relative per level (Q139).
- **PvP** gives both combat and non-combat perks, bounded for fairness (Q76).
- **Cosmetics, titles, animations and decorations** as milestone rewards (Q79), with lobby display (Q80).
- Masteries **persist through death, world reset and rebirth** (Q54).

**Modifiers**
- Each buff or perk is explicitly **additive or multiplicative** (Q78). Ordering: `final = floor(base × (1 + Σadditive) × Πmultiplicative)` (§33.3).

**Premium and boosters**
- **Roblox Premium** gives **+25% baseline XP** while the player is subscribed (Q150). Non-Premium players get no free baseline.
- Premium **stacks with** temporary consumable XP boosters that **every player can earn** (Q153). The two bonuses **add together**: +25% Premium and a +50% booster give +75% (Q162).

**Rebirth**
- Requires **both a general level and an endgame challenge** (Q81).
- **Resets general level to 1 only**. The vault, Permanent items, masteries, cosmetics and owned perks are kept. Coins, Gems and XP history are kept by default (Q82, §37.3).
- Rebirths get **progressively harder and more expensive** (Q83, Q85). Later rebirths **may award more tokens** (Q84).
- Tokens buy **permanently active perks**: XP, rare-drop luck, drop quantity, faster water movement. Some perks have multiple ranks; there are no loadout slots (Q86–88).
- **Respec** with **Gems, Robux or a redemption code**. Respec purchases are fulfilled in v0.14 (Q89).

The extraction receipts' reserved XP fields from v0.08 are now filled in.

## Security and anti-exploit

- Milestones count only server-verified actions. No AFK credit, alt-account PvP farming, cancel-and-refund craft spam or bot floods (§33.2 #6).
- The center bonus is granted once per receipt and never on retries (§24).
- Rebirth and perk purchases use operation IDs; no double token grants, and no vault deletion (§24).
- Premium status is checked on the server with `Player.MembershipType`, never trusted from the client (§38.1).

## UX and accessibility

- The rebirth preview lists **Reset**, **Kept** and **Gained** in plain words before confirming.
- Mastery panels show the next milestone and its reward.
- The extraction receipt separates base XP, credited XP and the conditional center bonus (§25.4.1).

## Validation

**Automated specs (§33.4, §37.4):**
- XP persists through death;
- the center bonus equals run XP once and never repeats;
- portable extraction gives no bonus;
- exactly five tracks capped at 10;
- the +1% per level yield reaches +10% at level 10;
- additive and multiplicative ordering: base 100, +10% and ×1.2 gives 132;
- rebirth resets only the level and keeps the vault, Permanent items, masteries and perks;
- perk ranks and token spending happen once.

**Studio checks:**
- Earn XP and die: the XP remains.
- A Center extraction shows the doubled XP.
- Reach a mastery milestone: the buff and cosmetic arrive once.
- Rebirth and buy a perk; the perk works.
- A Premium test account receives +25%.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] One modifier pipeline used by every XP and yield calculation.

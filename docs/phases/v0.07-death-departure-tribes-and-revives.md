# v0.07 — Death, departure, tribes and revives

> **Depends on:** v0.06 · **GDD sources:** §5.2–5.3, §21.2, §29 (all), §30 (all), §34.1 Q4–Q7, §35.1 Q32, §35.2 Q39–Q40, Q136, Q156–157, Q159c–f, Q161b–c

## Objective

Make losing meaningful and fair. Solo players die instantly; tribe members go down and can be revived. Any death or logout during active play leaves a 10-minute lootable body. Dead players are kept out of that world. Admin kicks always preserve items, and Permanent items never leave their owner on death.

## Player-facing result

- **Tribes:** create a tribe, invite, kick and form alliances, up to 8 members.
- A **solo** player at 0 HP dies at once. A **tribe member** goes **down** with a 20-HP downed pool and a 60-second bleed-out timer. They can crawl very slowly but cannot heal.
- **Anyone** can revive a downed player by holding interact for 10 seconds. Only one reviver at a time, and hitting the reviver does not stop it. A revived player gets 10% of max EHP back with no protection.
- On death, or **any logout after Play**, a **body** appears holding the player's ordinary items for **10 minutes**, lootable by anyone. The player is sent to the lobby and cannot rejoin that world.
- **Permanent items stay in the player's own inventory** on death and never appear in the body.
- **Admin kick** commands always save the player's items and show "Game update: your inventory was saved" on their next visit.

## Confirmed rules

**Tribes**
- **Max 8 members**; invite, kick and alliances (Q39).
- Tribes claim only player-built structures (claims arrive in v0.10).
- **No friendly fire** within a tribe (Q160b).

**Downed and revive**
- **Solo:** 0 HP = immediate death (Q32).
- **Tribe member:** 0 HP = downed with **20 downed HP**. Armor still applies to it, using the same EHP formula (Q68).
- **Bleed-out: 60 s of unpaused time.** A valid revive pauses it; cancelling resumes from the exact remaining time (§5.3).
- **Revive takes 10 continuous seconds** (Q68). Enemies may **finish a downed player at any time**, including mid-revive.
- **Anyone can revive**, including strangers (Q159c). **One reviver at a time** (Q159d).
- **Damage to the reviver does not stop the revive** (Q159e). It ends if the reviver cancels, leaves range, is downed or dies, or the downed player dies.
- **Downed players crawl very slowly and cannot heal**; a running food heal is cleared on downing (Q159f, §42.2 #3).
- **Revive restores 10% of max EHP** as actual HP (Q136). **No protection after revive** (Q159f).
- Downed players do **not** count as alive for extraction (§5.3 #8).

**Death and departure**
- Death creates **one** lootable body with the player's ordinary carried items, despawning with its remaining loot after exactly **600 s** from creation (§29).
- **Any logout during active play forfeits carried items** to a body: quitting, closing Roblox or losing connection (Q157). Loading, the world menu, failed admission, protected extraction and maintenance kicks are exceptions.
- A dead player is **kicked to the lobby** and **cannot rejoin that world** while it runs (Q5). A surviving player who disconnected may rejoin, but their dropped items are not restored (Q4).
- **Permanent items stay in the owner's backpack/hotbar on death**, with no reclaim step or fee (Q156). Admin safe kicks keep Permanent items too.
- A whole-server **crash mid-fight should return items where possible** (Q157). The mechanism comes in v0.17. This phase must not create fake corpses in a server that no longer exists.

**Admin kicks**
- Admin kick commands **only ever perform a safe kick** (Q161b). The player's exact held items are saved to maintenance escrow, they get no extraction payout or Forge eligibility, and on their next Play they continue with those same items (§30).

## Data and persistence

- `profile.receipts.maintenance[operationId]`: the exact item snapshot, status and the update version. Consumed once on the next successful Play.
- `profile.permanentItems` stays authoritative for ownership. Death changes nothing about it.
- The JobId exclusion lists live in server memory and in matchmaking (MemoryStore with a TTL as long as the world lives).

## Security and anti-exploit

- One body per death or departure, even if callbacks run twice or the player rejoins (§29.2 #7).
- Admin permission comes from a server-held allowlist, never a client flag or kick-reason string (§30.3).
- A client cannot fake a maintenance kick by disconnecting.
- Death and extraction are mutually exclusive commits (§24): a player cannot both drop a body and extract the same items.
- Revive requests are checked for range, the single-reviver lock, a live downed target and continuous presence.

## UX and accessibility

- The downed screen shows a large timer (with "paused" while a revive runs), crawl controls and who is reviving.
- The body prompt and its remaining time are readable on mobile and controller. Deaths are not announced server-wide (§29.2 #6).
- The death summary separates **Dropped (lootable)**, **Safe in vault** and **Permanent: kept**.
- After a safe kick: "Game update: your inventory was saved" in the lobby and world menu.

## Validation

**Automated specs (`DownedRules`, departure state):**
- 60 s bleed-out pauses and resumes exactly;
- revive completes at 10 s;
- a second reviver is rejected;
- damage to the reviver does not cancel;
- revived HP is 10 and 15 for 100 and 150 max HP in full iron;
- one body per death;
- Permanent items are excluded from body contents;
- a maintenance snapshot is consumed once.

**Studio checks (GDD §29, §30, §34.4, §35.4):**
- Solo death leaves a body, ejects to the lobby and blocks rejoin.
- A downed tribe member is revived by a stranger.
- A downed player is finished mid-revive.
- Quit during combat leaves a body; quit from the menu does not.
- An admin safe-kick mid-fight saves items, then Play restores them once.
- The body despawns at 600 s with its remaining loot.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Tribes, downed and revive work exactly as confirmed.
- [ ] Every active-play exit produces exactly one 10-minute body.
- [ ] Dead players cannot rejoin the same world; parties regroup in the lobby.
- [ ] Admin kicks are always safe kicks and restore exactly once.
- [ ] Permanent items never leave their owner on death.

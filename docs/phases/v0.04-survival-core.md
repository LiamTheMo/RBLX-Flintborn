# v0.04 — Survival core: health, stamina, food and healing

> **Depends on:** v0.03 · **GDD sources:** §1.2, §4.3, §4.5, §5.4, §21.1, §37.1 Q61–65, Q135, Q137, Q160c–d

## Objective

Give an active character the three launch survival meters (health, stamina and food), with sprint and crouch movement, instant medicine and food heal-over-time. All of it is server-authoritative and matches the confirmed numbers exactly.

## Player-facing result

- A HUD with health, stamina and food bars, plus medicine-cooldown and food-healing indicators.
- **Sprint** for up to 3 seconds; stamina refills from empty in 5 seconds. **Crouch** at any time without stamina.
- Food drains from full to empty in 5 minutes. Below 25% the player moves 10% slower; nothing worse happens at 0%.
- **Medicine** heals instantly (+10 / +25 / +50), then plays a 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 second animation during which no other medicine can be used.
- **Food** restores hunger and heals over 10 seconds. Getting hit halves the healing still to come and cancels it once 10 HP or less would remain. Several food heals and medicine can run together.

## Confirmed rules

| Rule | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survival meters | Health, stamina, food **only**: no thirst, temperature, mana or durability | §4.3, Q25 |
| Base max HP | **100**; gear and approved upgrades can raise it | Q61 |
| Sprint | **3 s** on a full bar; **5 s** to refill from empty; **only sprint** spends stamina | Q62 |
| Food drain | **100% → 0% in 300 s** | Q63 |
| Hunger slow | **−10% speed strictly below 25%**; exactly 25% is not slowed | Q63, §37.4 #3 |
| 0% food | **No extra effect** beyond the slow; no starvation damage | Q160c |
| Medicine | Basic **+10**, Advanced **+25**, Potion **+50** HP, applied **immediately**, capped at current max HP | Q64 |
| Medicine cooldown | Animation **0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 s** is the **only** cooldown | Q135 |
| Food heal | Item-specific total over **10 s** | Q65 |
| Food interruption | Each hit **halves remaining** food healing; if the halved remainder is **≤10 HP**, cancel it | Q65 |
| Food examples | Berries **+10% hunger, +10 HP**; Cooked Meat **+35% hunger, +30 HP** | Q137 |
| Stacking | Food heals **stack** with each other and with medicine | Q160d |
| Downed players | Cannot heal; a running food heal is cleared on downing | Q159f (enforced in v0.07) |

The shared formulas already exist in `GameConstants` (`FoodHealAfterHit`, `IsFoodSlowed`) and are covered by specs.

## Systems and modules

| Path | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `src/server/Services/SurvivalService.luau` | Per-player health, stamina and food state; drain ticks; movement-speed modifiers; replication to the owner |
| `src/server/Services/HealingService.luau` | Validates and applies medicine (consume one, heal, start animation lock) and food (consume, add hunger, start a heal-over-time entry) |
| `src/shared/Survival/SurvivalRules.luau` | Pure step functions: stamina drain and refill, food drain, speed multiplier, heal clamping, food heal ticks and hit interruption across several active heals |
| `src/shared/Items/Consumables.luau` | Catalog of medicine and food items (heal, hunger, animation) referenced by item ID |
| `src/client/Controllers/SurvivalHudController.luau` | Bars, cooldown ring, food-heal indicator |
| `src/client/Controllers/MovementController.luau` | Sprint/crouch input for keyboard, gamepad and touch. Sends intent; the server owns stamina and speed |

**Server tick:** one `Heartbeat`-driven update for all active players, in a single loop with a `pcall` per player (as in Fuse-Factory's SessionService). Food heals apply in small increments (placeholder 0.25 s) so interruptions are precise.

**Several food heals:** each active entry tracks `remaining` and `endsAt`. A damaging hit applies `FoodHealAfterHit` to **each** entry separately. Applied healing is never taken back.

## Data and persistence

Survival meters are **run state**, not profile state: they reset on each new spawn. Consumables are inventory items (v0.05). Nothing here writes to DataStores.

## Security and anti-exploit

- Medicine requests during the animation lock are ignored and consume nothing (§4.5).
- The client never reports HP, stamina or food. Only movement intent and use requests are sent.
- Sprint speed is applied by the server through the humanoid. Speed checks against impossible positions come in v0.17.
- Repeated or replayed use remotes are idempotent per request ID.

## UX and accessibility

- Bars show numbers on hover/focus and use shape plus colour for low states.
- The medicine cooldown is a visible ring on the item slot. The food heal shows remaining amount and time.
- Touch has dedicated sprint and crouch buttons; controller uses standard bindings.

## Validation

**Automated specs (`SurvivalRules`):**
- sprint lasts exactly 3 s from full and refills in 5 s;
- food reaches 0 at 300 s;
- slow triggers at 24.99% but not at 25%;
- medicine clamps to max HP;
- 40 → 20 → cancelled food interruption;
- two food heals both halve on one hit;
- medicine during a food heal applies both.

**Studio checks:**
- Spam the medicine key during the animation: only one heal and one item consumed.
- Take hits during food healing: the remaining amount halves visibly and cancels at ≤10.
- Starve to 0%: no damage, only the slow.
- Test on desktop, touch emulator and gamepad.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] All confirmed values above enforced by the server and covered by specs.
- [ ] HUD readable on all three input types.
- [ ] No stamina use for melee, bows, crouch, gathering or building.

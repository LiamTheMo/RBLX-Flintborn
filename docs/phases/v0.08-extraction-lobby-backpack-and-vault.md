# v0.08 — Extraction, lobby backpack and secure vault

> **Depends on:** v0.07 · **GDD sources:** §2.2, §9 (all), §10.3, §22.1–22.2, §31.2, §34.3 Q17–24, §39.2 Q126–131, §40.2, Q151–155, Q158, Q161a

## Objective

Close the core loop: **risk → loot → extract → keep**. Players start a shared extraction countdown at a portal, survive until it ends while standing in its zone, enter a protected animation and arrive in the lobby. Ordinary materials go to the secure vault; everything else goes to a durable lobby backpack. This is the first fully playable vertical slice.

## Player-facing result

- Each world has a server-specific subset of **fixed extraction portals** at known POIs. Clicking an available portal starts a **120-second countdown** that everyone nearby hears and sees from far away.
- Players may roam, hide, fight and heal during the countdown. Only living players **inside the zone at zero** extract, including strangers and enemies.
- Extracting players freeze in an **invulnerable animation**; disconnecting from that moment on is safe. They then arrive in the **lobby** with a receipt.
- A rare **portable rune** can be placed almost anywhere (never inside a structure) to start a **30-second** extraction for everyone who fits in its zone.
- The **Center Crater portal doubles every extracted material**. Portable runes never double, even inside the crater.
- In the lobby: **ordinary materials are already in the vault**; gear, tools, special materials and other items sit in the **lobby backpack**, which persists until the player vaults or redeploys them.
- The **vault** shows 25 item slots and material stacks up to 1,000 each. Anything over a limit is deleted and listed on the receipt.

## Confirmed rules

**Countdown and zone**
- Fixed exits: **one server-shared 120 s timer** per activation (Q21). The clock ignores who is nearby, damage, healing and deaths (Q18–19).
- **Presence counts only at the deadline**: alive, eligible and inside the small zone at expiry (Q17). There is no continuous stay requirement.
- **Strangers may use any extraction**; the **activator's death does not matter** (Q158).
- **Zone radius is authored per site** (Q158).
- **Large-radius audio and visual alert** on activation (Q20).
- **Candidate portal POIs are fixed**; each server activates a **different subset** (Q22).
- **50 m no-build aura** around every fixed portal and rune (§3.1). Enforced in building (v0.10); the zones are defined here.

**Portable runes**
- **30 s countdown anywhere except inside structures** (Q22, Q158).
- **Everyone who fits in the zone** may use it (Q158).
- **Never grants the 2× bonus** (Q158).

**Protection and destination**
- From the **server-authorised animation start**, players are **invulnerable and disconnect-safe**; locked items still settle (§9.1, §22.2).
- Destination: the **separate lobby**, not the world menu (Q24).
- **No Coins are awarded for extracting** (Q118). The Center portal multiplies **materials (ordinary and special) and run XP**, never Coins or Gems (Q119, Q23).
- **Unique equipment is never multiplied**; one sword stays one sword (§9.3). Event items are handled **per item** by their catalog entry (stackable material vs unique instance) and carry an `"event"` tag (Q161a).

**Where items go after settlement**
- **Ordinary materials auto-bank** into vault material storage (Q129).
- **Equipment, tools, other items and special materials** go to the **lobby backpack**, which **persists across logout, rejoin, lobby server changes and world resets** (Q152).
- **Vault material storage: 1,000 units per stack**; any excess is **deleted** (Q151, Q154). Example: 600 Stone at the Center → 1,200 → 1,000 stored, 200 deleted.
- **Lobby backpack: 50 slots**; incoming items beyond that are **deleted** (Q154).
- **Settlement failures** (DataStore error, disconnect, retry) never delete anything; they stay pending and reconcile once (§41.2 #1).
- **Vault deposits are manual** for non-material items. The **item vault starts at 25 slots** (Q96); upgrades arrive in v0.14. A full vault blocks the deposit rather than deleting.
- **Pressing Play deploys every unvaulted lobby item** (Q155; built in v0.03).

## Systems and modules

| Path | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `src/server/Services/ExtractionService.luau` | Portal activation, shared timers, alerts, deadline snapshot, protected animation, per-player receipts |
| `src/server/Services/VaultService.luau` | Material storage (per-stack 1,000 cap, overflow deletion), 25-slot item vault, manual deposit/withdraw |
| `src/server/Services/LobbyBackpackService.luau` | Durable 50-slot lobby backpack; overflow deletion; hands items to `LoadoutService` on Play |
| `src/server/Services/PortalSiteService.luau` | Candidate portal POIs, per-server active subset, authored zone radii, no-build zones exposed to BuildingService |
| `src/shared/Extraction/ExtractionRules.luau` | Pure rules: deadline qualification, center multiplier eligibility (fixed Center portal only), receipt routing, overflow settlement (uses `GameConstants.SettleMaterialStack`) |
| `src/shared/Extraction/PortalCatalog.luau` | Portal definitions: ID, POI, kind (`fixed`, `center`), zone radius, alert radius |
| `src/client/Controllers/ExtractionController.luau` | Countdown UI, zone indicator, "Protected: extracting" transition |
| `src/client/Controllers/VaultController.luau` | Lobby vault and backpack UI, deposit/withdraw, receipt screen |

**Settlement pipeline (GDD §9.2):**
1. At expiry, snapshot alive in-zone players and set them to `ExtractionProtected`.
2. Lock each player's exact inventory and create a receipt with an operation ID.
3. Compute center doubling once.
4. Route ordinary materials to the vault and everything else to the lobby backpack.
5. Apply capacity limits, deleting overflow and recording it on the receipt.
6. Commit through `OperationJournal`.
7. Teleport to the lobby.

## Data and persistence

- `profile.receipts.extraction[receiptId]`:
  - `runId`, `portalId`, `portalKind`;
  - `baseMaterialStacks`, `centerMultiplier`, `bonusStacks`;
  - `deletedOverflow`;
  - `itemsToBackpack`;
  - XP fields reserved for v0.13: `runGeneralXPEarned`, `runMasteryXPEarned`, `centerBonusGeneralXP`, `centerBonusMasteryXP`;
  - `settlementStatus`.
- `profile.vault.materials[catalogId] = quantity` (≤ 1,000).
- `profile.vault.items` (up to the slot count).
- `profile.lobbyBackpack` (≤ 50 slots).

## Security and anti-exploit

From GDD §24 and §12.2:

- one receipt per player per portal event, so a replay never credits twice;
- death and extraction are mutually exclusive commits;
- no late entry after the snapshot;
- nobody can extract someone else's items;
- the center bonus is checked on the server from `portalKind == "center"`, never from client position claims or rune type;
- rune placement is checked on the server against structure volumes and other runes;
- the client animation alone is never proof of extraction.

## UX and accessibility

- The countdown shows portal name, time left, whether the player is in the zone and the projected bonus (2× only at the Center).
- The text changes from **"120-second danger"** to **"Protected: extracting"** at animation start (§31.6).
- Receipt (§25.2): portal, base and doubled materials, unique items listed separately, **deleted overflow**, recipe progress and the next action.
- Vault and backpack screens make the difference clear: **Vault = safe forever, Backpack = goes with you on Play**.

## Validation

**Automated specs (`ExtractionRules`):**
- a player outside at zero does not qualify; one who arrives late but is inside at zero does;
- downed or dead players are excluded;
- only the fixed Center portal doubles;
- unique items stay single;
- 600 Stone → 1,000 stored and 200 deleted;
- 55 items → 50 kept and 5 deleted;
- receipt replay is a no-op;
- a settlement failure deletes nothing.

**Studio checks (GDD §34.4 #8–14, §39.6):**
- Two activators at one portal share one timer.
- A stranger extracts on an enemy's timer.
- The activator dies and the rest still extract.
- Disconnect one frame after the animation starts: items settle.
- A portable rune refuses placement inside a building.
- A Center extraction doubles materials but not a sword.
- Lobby backpack items survive logout and a world reset.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] A tester can spawn, gather, craft, fight, extract and see items in the lobby; the core loop is playable.
- [ ] Every confirmed rule above enforced on the server with spec coverage.
- [ ] Overflow deletion appears on receipts and never results from technical failure.
- [ ] Lobby backpack and vault persist across logout and world reset.

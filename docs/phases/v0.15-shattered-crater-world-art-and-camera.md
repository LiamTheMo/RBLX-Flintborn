# v0.15 — Shattered Crater world, art direction and camera

> **Depends on:** v0.11 (event placement), v0.12 (Guardian arena) · **GDD sources:** §3 (all), §5.1 safe POIs, §18.1, §36.5, Q33, Q57–58, Q160a

## Objective

Replace placeholder terrain with the real **Shattered Crater**. It is one seamless island with three concentric risk regions, biome-specific resources, the fixed portal POIs, the designated safe-zone POIs and the Guardian caldera. Everything is built in the confirmed primitive, blocky Booga Booga style with more detail, and seen through an adjustable camera.

## Player-facing result

- **Outer Rim:** beaches, woodland, starter resources, hidden minor extraction runes.
- **Inner Ring:** dense jungle, dangerous fauna, obsidian and jade, contested approaches.
- **Center Crater:** an open volcanic caldera with little cover, the Guardian and the central portal.
- Recognisable landmarks (giant roots, ruins, monoliths, the volcanic ridge) for navigation without a minimap.
- Several routes between the rings with jungle sightline breaks.
- Specific **safe-zone POIs**, clearly marked.
- An **adjustable camera** with zoom.

## Confirmed rules

- **One seamless island, three concentric regions:** Outer Rim (low risk, not PvP-immune), Inner Ring (medium), Center Crater (extreme) (§3).
- **Random spawn anywhere** valid on the map (Q33). Spawn candidates are spread across the whole island (§3.2).
- **Fixed, recognisable portal POIs**, with a different active subset per server (Q22).
- **Safe POIs are specific locations designed here** (Q160a). They are visibly marked and server-enforced (§3.2).
- **50 m no-build aura** around portals (§3.1).
- **Jade and obsidian come from the Inner Ring** (§18.1).
- **Art:** **primitive, blocky Booga Booga look** with **more detail in the map, structures and equipment** (Q57). Visual clutter must never hide projectiles, gathering nodes, totem influence, rare loot or extraction signals (§36.5).
- **Camera:** **adjustable** by the user (Q58). Bounds must not create corner-peeking advantages in PvP (§36.5).
- **30-player world** sized for that population (Q1).

## Systems and modules

| Path | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `src/maps/ShatteredCrater/` | Map models synced to `ServerStorage.Maps` (or Studio-authored and published from Studio, since the release pipeline ships scripts only) |
| `src/server/Services/WorldBuildService.luau` | Loads the map, tags biomes, registers resource node anchors, spawn candidates, portal POIs and safe zones from tagged parts |
| `src/shared/World/BiomeCatalog.luau` | Biomes with region, resource pools, enemy pools and event eligibility |
| `src/shared/World/PoiCatalog.luau` | Named POIs: portal candidates, safe zones, ritual sites, Guardian arena |
| `src/client/Controllers/CameraController.luau` | Adjustable zoom with min/max bounds; touch pinch and controller support; saved preference |
| `src/client/Controllers/MapController.luau` | Compass and map with landmarks, events and portals |

**Authoring:**
- Use CollectionService tags (`SpawnCandidate`, `ResourceAnchor:<biome>`, `PortalSite:<id>`, `SafeZone:<id>`, `RitualSite`, `GuardianArena`) so gameplay reads the map without hard-coded coordinates.
- Large geometry is authored in Studio, and saved to the place from Studio, because the GitHub release ships scripts only (`docs/roblox-live-deployment.md`).

## Data and persistence

- `profile.choices.camera` stores zoom preferences.
- Everything else is world content.

## Security and anti-exploit

- Safe-zone volumes and portal zones are server-side shapes.
- Spawn candidates are filtered on the server for terrain, hazards and crowding (§3.2).
- Map-out-of-bounds and unreachable clip spots are blocked.

## UX and accessibility

- Landmarks are visible from a distance on low graphics settings.
- Safe zones have a distinct, colour-blind-safe boundary effect and HUD text.
- Camera zoom works with mouse wheel, pinch and right stick. A first-person option is Open; start with third-person only.

## Validation

**Automated specs:**
- every portal POI has an authored zone radius and is inside its region;
- every biome has at least one resource pool;
- every safe zone is tagged and has a volume;
- spawn candidates exist in all three regions.

**Studio and live checks:**
- Traverse every ring on mobile, measuring travel times.
- 30-player server performance profile on real mobile devices (§12.3).
- Readability pass: projectiles, nodes and loot visible against every biome.
- Camera bounds tested for PvP fairness.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Island with all three regions, landmarks, portal POIs, safe POIs and Guardian caldera published.
- [ ] Biome-specific resources and events placed through tags.
- [ ] Adjustable camera with saved preferences.
- [ ] Performance target met on mid-range mobile with 30 players.

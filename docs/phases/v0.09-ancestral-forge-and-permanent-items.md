# v0.09 — Ancestral Forge and Permanent items

> **Depends on:** v0.08 · **GDD sources:** §15 (all), §16 (all), §17 (all), §18, §19.3, §22.1, §23.3, §24, §34.2 Q8–Q16, §38.2 Q109–110, Q149, Q156

## Objective

Give extracted loot long-term value. Players spend vaulted materials at the secure **Ancestral Forge** on special recipes and convert eligible items into **Permanent** items: they survive the owner's death, keep exactly their stats, and can still change hands by being dropped or traded.

## Player-facing result

- A **Forge** in the lobby with recipe categories, Owned/Needed counts from the **vault only**, and recipe unlock progress.
- **Ordinary special recipes** that turn extracted materials into stronger ordinary (non-magical) gear, medicine, food or arrows at default quality.
- **Permanent conversion** of a designated vaulted item, starting with the **basic pickaxe**. A confirmation screen names the exact item being transformed and the ingredients spent.
- Permanent items show a clear badge, survive death (they stay in the backpack/hotbar), and can be **dropped** for someone else to pick up, which transfers ownership.

## Confirmed rules

**What a Permanent item is**
- **Survives the owner's death** and stays in their backpack/hotbar, with **no reclaim step or fee** (Q8, Q156).
- **Same combat stats as the source**; conversion adds no power (Q11). The source's **±5% quality roll is preserved, never re-rolled** (§17.3 #4 Confirmed).
- **Transferable:** deliberately dropping it and another player picking it up **transfers ownership**. **Trading** transfers it too (Q13; lobby trading arrives in v0.14). Killing the wearer never transfers it (§17.1).
- **No limit** on how many Permanent items a player owns or carries (Q156).
- A dropped Permanent item **left unclaimed when the world resets is lost** (Q156).

**Who can convert what**
- **Only designated item archetypes** can convert; rarity alone grants nothing (Q12, Q109).
- The source must be **owned, vaulted, eligible, unlocked, not deployed and not already Permanent** (§15.3). Converting **consumes the source**; it never leaves both an ordinary copy and a Permanent copy (§15.3).
- **First conversion target:** the **basic pickaxe**, after roughly **a couple of successful extractions plus one rare event** (Q110, Q149). A pickaxe is a tool; converting it adds no mining stats.
- **Recipes unlock through player level and discovery** (Q15). **High-tier recipes need materials from several activities and sources** (Q16). The **rarest Permanent gear cannot come from farming/building alone** (Q55).

**The Forge itself**
- **Lobby crafting gives default quality**, with no ±5% roll (Q105).
- **No random failure** that destroys ingredients: valid and fully funded means 100% success (§16.4).
- **No player magic or enchantments at launch** (§4.4).

## Systems and modules

| Path | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `src/server/Services/ForgeService.luau` | Recipe validation against vault contents, reservation, commit and receipts; only accepts sources in the vault |
| `src/server/Services/PermanentItemService.luau` | Canonical Permanent records: owner, location, ownership version; drop, pickup, death retention, world-reset deletion of unclaimed drops |
| `src/server/Services/RecipeUnlockService.luau` | Tracks discoveries and level-gated unlocks; grants recipe knowledge once |
| `src/shared/Crafting/ForgeRecipes.luau` | Special and conversion recipes with versions, inputs, source eligibility and unlock requirements |
| `src/shared/Items/PermanentRules.luau` | Pure eligibility checks and the item-location state machine for Permanent items (§22.1) |
| `src/client/Controllers/ForgeController.luau` | Recipe browser, tracker, pinned recipes, conversion confirmation (§25.3) |

**Operation flow (GDD §16.3, §23.3):**
1. Request: `recipeId`, `sourceId`, `nonce`.
2. Reserve: lock the source and the input stacks.
3. Validate: version, unlock and capacity.
4. Commit: consume inputs and the source, create one output.
5. Return the receipt.

A retry with the same nonce returns the same receipt. Any pending operation must reconcile before the next one.

**Permanent item locations:**
- `PermanentVaulted`
- `PermanentCarried`
- `PermanentEquipped`
- `PermanentWorldDropped`
- `PermanentTradeEscrow`

An item is in exactly one of these at any moment.

## Data and persistence

- `profile.permanentItems[instanceId]`: `catalogId`, `qualityRoll`, `currentOwnerId`, `location`, `ownershipVersion`, `provenance`.
- `profile.recipes.unlocked[recipeId]`: `unlockSource` and `unlockedAt`.
- `profile.receipts.forge[operationId]`: inputs, source, output, `recipeVersion`, status.
- **Cross-account transfer** on pickup: the item gets a new `ownershipVersion`, and the former owner's record is retired through an operation journal entry, so the old owner cannot restore it (§17.2).

## Security and anti-exploit

From GDD §24 (Forge rows):

- client-invented IDs, a source used in two requests, converting a deployed item, client-edited ingredient counts, mid-teleport consumption and changed rarity previews are all rejected;
- two simultaneous conversions of one item: only one commits;
- a dropped Permanent item can be claimed once only;
- a disconnect after dropping never restores a copy to the former owner;
- admin restore tools require audited unique restoration IDs (§24).

## UX and accessibility

- Forge filters: tools, weapons, armor, food and medicine, construction, Permanent.
- Recipe percentages count only vaulted materials; backpack items never count (§25.3).
- The conversion confirmation names the exact source item and the Permanent rules (kept on death; transferable by drop or trade). Confirming is deliberate on touch and controller.
- The vocabulary stays consistent: **Vaulted** = stored safely, **Permanent** = conversion class (§15.2). Playtests must confirm testers can explain the difference (§27.2).

## Validation

**Automated specs (GDD §27.1 #4–10):**
- conversion charges each input once, consumes the source and creates one Permanent record;
- an item that isn't yours, isn't vaulted, is already Permanent, is deployed or is ineligible is rejected;
- two concurrent conversions of one item yield one;
- a retry after a timeout gives the same final state;
- death keeps Permanent items, including several at once;
- drop then pickup transfers ownership once;
- unclaimed drops are deleted on world reset;
- the quality roll is preserved.

**Studio checks:**
- Earn the pickaxe conversion in about two extractions plus one rare event.
- Die wearing several Permanent items and keep them all.
- Drop a Permanent sword; a second player picks it up and owns it.
- The original owner cannot recover it after rejoining.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Forge special recipes and Permanent pickaxe conversion live.
- [ ] Every Permanent rule above holds and is covered by specs.
- [ ] Single-instance ownership proven across drop, pickup, death, rejoin and world reset.

# v0.14 — Economy, shops, trading and monetization

> **Depends on:** v0.13 · **GDD sources:** §1.2 monetization rule, §10 (all), §18.2, §36.1–36.2, §38.1 Q90–99, §39.1 Q116–125, Q130, Q150, Q153, Q89

## Objective

Make the account economy work:
- **Coins** from quests and fixed-price NPC sales, spent in NPC shops;
- **Gems** from quests, milestones, achievements, rebirths, rare encounters and optional Robux;
- **lobby-only direct trading** of eligible items and Gems;
- **vault upgrades**;
- monetization that sells only convenience or speed, always with an earnable equivalent.

## Player-facing result

- A **Coin** and **Gem** wallet in the lobby. Neither ever drops on death.
- **NPC merchants** that buy materials, monster drops and crafted gear at fixed prices. Selling something a Forge recipe needs triggers a warning.
- **NPC shops** for starter food, medicine and arrows, seeds, basic tools, cosmetics and decorations.
- **Quests** paying **250 / 1,000 / 3,000 Coins** (easy/normal/hard) and Gem rewards around **25**.
- **Vault upgrades**: +25 item slots per tier, at the same Gem price every tier or an equivalent Robux purchase.
- **Lobby trading**: a two-player locked offer screen with eligible items and Gems. Coins never.
- **Premium** subscribers see their +25% XP. Everyone can earn XP boosters. Rebirth perk respecs can be bought with Gems, Robux or a code.

## Confirmed rules

**Coins**
- Coins come from **quests** and **fixed-price NPC sales** (Q90). There is **no Coin reward for extracting** (Q118) and **no Center multiplier on Coins or Gems** (Q119).
- **Quest Coin bands: easy 250, normal 1,000, hard 3,000** (Q117).
- **NPC shops must sell items** (Q116). The catalog ideas in §10.1 are proposals.
- **NPC merchants buy** materials, monster drops and crafted gear, **including rare materials**, at **fixed prices** (Q95, Q120).
- **Auto-sell** of useless materials is **not enabled**. If ever built, it is opt-in with an allowlist and a receipt (Q118, §39.1).

**Gems**
- Gems come from quests, milestones, achievements, rebirths, rare enemies/resources and optional Robux (Q91). A **typical grant is ~25** (Q121).
- **Earning targets: ~10,000 Coins and ~100 Gems per active hour** (Q92).
- **Coin → Gem conversion: not built** (Q93, Q124).
- **Neither currency ever drops** (Q94).

**Vault upgrades**
- **25 starting slots**, **+25 per upgrade** (Q96). The **Gem price is the same for every tier** (Q122); an equivalent Robux purchase is also offered.

**Trading**
- **Lobby only**: **eligible items and Gems**, **never Coins** (Q98).
- **Only designated items** can trade (Q130). **Raw materials cannot**, except catalog-flagged rare or Exclusive ones (Q56). Permanent items can trade (Q13).
- Gem trading has **restrictions**: level minimum, limits and/or fees (Q125).
- There is **no marketplace or auction** (Q50).

**Monetization**
- **Every paid benefit has an earnable in-game route** (§1.2, §36.2).
- **Robux never buys direct PvP damage, armor or health** (Q99).
- A meaningful premium-equivalent purchase takes **~10 active hours** to earn (Q97, Q123).
- **Premium +25% XP** stacks with earnable **temporary XP boosters** (Q150, Q153).
- **Perk respec** via Gems, Robux or a code (Q89).
- **Paid random items** must follow Roblox's policy, including odds disclosure (§38.1).

## Data and persistence

- `profile.currency`: `coins`, `gems`, `currencyGrantLedger`, `npcSaleReceipts`, `purchaseEntitlements`.
- `profile.vault.upgradeTier`.
- `profile.quests`.
- Trade records (`directTradeId`, participants, locked offers, eligibility snapshot, outcome) stored on both profiles, settled through operation journals.
- Cross-server trades are not supported: both players must be in the same lobby server.

## Security and anti-exploit

- Coin and Gem amounts cannot be forged (§27.1 #33).
- Purchase receipts are processed exactly once, even on retries and server restarts.
- A trade interrupted between the two writes recovers to one outcome; there are no double grants (§24).
- Non-eligible materials are rejected even if both players accept.
- Alt-account Gem laundering is limited by trade restrictions (§10.2).
- Merchant sales of rare materials are allowed but warned about; repeat-sale exploits are excluded from earning analytics (§38.4).

## UX and accessibility

- Every Robux offer shows its **earnable equivalent** next to it (§25.4.1).
- The trade screen shows both offers, a lock, a confirm countdown and clear cancel protection. **Coins are not offered** in the trade UI.
- Merchant quotes show item value and warn on Permanent-recipe ingredients.

## Validation

**Automated specs:**
- quest bands are 250/1,000/3,000;
- extraction grants zero Coins;
- the vault adds exactly 25 per upgrade at the same Gem price every tier;
- trade rules reject Coins and unflagged raw materials and allow flagged materials, Gems and Permanent items;
- every Robux offer in `OfferCatalog` has an earnable equivalent;
- the receipt processor is idempotent.

**Studio and live checks:**
- Buy from a shop and sell to a merchant.
- Complete each quest tier once.
- Trade a Permanent item for Gems between two accounts, disconnecting mid-trade and recovering.
- Buy a vault upgrade with Gems and with Robux in a test place.
- A Premium account receives +25% XP.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Coins, Gems, shops, merchants and quests live and persistent.
- [ ] Lobby trading safe against disconnects and duplication.
- [ ] Every monetized benefit has an earnable path, enforced by a spec.
- [ ] Roblox purchase policy (paid random items, Premium) reviewed before release.

# v0.12 — Bosses: Guardian and scalable encounters

> **Depends on:** v0.11 · **GDD sources:** §3 (Center Crater), §7.2, §8 (all), §20.3, §35.3 Q45–46, §38.3 Q115, §39.4 Q147–148

## Objective

Give the world its headline fights. The **Guardian** in the Center Crater appears every 15–20 minutes, has a pillar phase and drops a Legendary at 25% HP. Event, ritual and structure bosses use fixed baselines that scale with the active population's combined level and gear. Every reward stays contested until extracted.

## Player-facing result

- A **Horn of War** warning **3 minutes** before the Guardian appears in the Center Crater.
- **Phase I:** the Guardian is invulnerable until players destroy its **energy pillars**.
- **Phase II:** melee, slow projectiles and area attacks.
- **Phase III:** at **25% HP** it releases a **Legendary item** on the ground, once, before it dies.
- **Event bosses** from the director, **ritual bosses** summoned by placing rare materials, and **structure bosses** inside generated ruins and camps.
- Boss loot is physical and lootable by anyone. Nothing is safe until extracted.

## Confirmed rules

**Guardian**
- About every **15–20 minutes**, with a **3-minute** Horn of War warning (§8).
- Pillar phase, then gauntlet, then a **physical Legendary at 25% HP, once per encounter** (§8.1, §12.2 invariant 8).
- **Can destroy player-built structures near its arena**, so structures cannot be used to cheese it (§3.1).
- **Fully contested by players at every stage**; it is not a private room (§8.1). Pillar shielding is boss-only, not a player shield.

**Other bosses**
- **Three sources:** event (weighted director), ritual (player-placed rare materials) and structure (generated ruins/camps) (Q45). A ritual summon is a world interaction, not player magic (§7.2).
- **Reset by spawn type:** event rules, ritual rules, structure scripts; the Guardian keeps its own cycle (Q46, §8.3).
- **Fixed authored baseline** health, damage and difficulty per boss (Q147).
- **Scaling** uses a **combined player level + equipped gear strength** signal from the active-world population (Q148), resistant to gear removal and low-level alt joins (§8.2).

**Loot**
- **No automatic boss rewards** make loot safe. Drops stay contested until extraction (§8.2).
- The Legendary is ordinary world loot until extracted; it cannot become Permanent in the crater (§15.3).

## Data and persistence

None persistent. Encounter IDs scope each once-only grant. Kill and participation telemetry feeds v0.17. XP for boss participation comes through v0.13 when added.

## Security and anti-exploit

- The 25% Legendary grant is keyed to the encounter ID, so crossing 25% HP twice cannot duplicate it (§24).
- The scaling snapshot is taken **at spawn** and ignores later gear removal or alt joins (§8.2).
- Ritual materials are consumed once on the server.
- Arena structure clearing applies only near the Guardian arena.

## UX and accessibility

- The Guardian countdown is distinct from cache beacons (§25.4).
- Phase changes are telegraphed with UI, VFX and audio, and stay readable under heavy particles on mobile (§27.2).
- The 25% drop gets a clear "Legendary released" cue and a carrier highlight (burden of wealth, §25.4).

## Validation

**Automated specs:**
- Guardian scheduling stays inside 15–20 min with a 3 min warning;
- the 25% grant happens once per encounter ID;
- scaling from a combined score is clamped and ignores changes after the snapshot;
- each boss source resets by its own rule.

**Studio checks (GDD §27.2, §20.3):**
- Two tribes contest the pillars.
- The Legendary drops at 25% and is stolen and extracted.
- Building near the arena gets destroyed.
- A ritual summon is interrupted by a third party.
- Run with a full server for performance.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Guardian full encounter live with the once-only 25% Legendary.
- [ ] Event, ritual and structure bosses spawn and reset by type.
- [ ] Combined level + gear scaling implemented with clamps and a snapshot at spawn.
